🤖 Chatbot Endpoints & Placeholders

  • GET /api/current?format={artist} - {title}
  • GET /api/playnext?url=<url> (Song requests)
  • GET /api/blacklist?url=<url> (Ban & skip)
  • Placeholders: {artist}, {title}, {album}, {duration}, {url}
