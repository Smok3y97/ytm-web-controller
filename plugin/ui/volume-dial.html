Volume Dial Settings
Step Size (%)
Percentage amount adjusted per dial tick (1% – 25%, Default: 5%).
Title Template
Placeholders: {artist}, {title}, {album} (or static title like YouTube Music Volume)
Controls
• Rotate Clockwise: Volume Up
• Rotate Counter-Clockwise: Volume Down
